03 / A LITTLE MORE ABOUT ME

A name with meaning.

Spaced-out Sea Otter is not only a translation of a name. It is a translation of the character itself.

얼빵

→ SPACED-OUT

Rather than romanizing ‘얼빵’ as Eolbbang, it was translated as ‘Spaced-out’ — slightly absent-minded, a little offbeat, and endearing. The name itself introduces the personality.

해달

→ SUN + MOON

In Korean, ‘해달’ (haedal) means sea otter. But it can also be read as 해 (sun) + 달 (moon) — one name carrying two layers: the animal, and the sky.

05 / IRWOROBONGDAN

Not alone.

Meet the friends of
Irworobongdan.

Irworobongdan (일월오봉단) is Shin Haedal’s original character group, inspired by Irworobongdo (일월오봉도, Sun, Moon and Five Peaks). This traditional Korean painting motif depicts the sun, moon, five mountain peaks, pine trees and water, and is closely associated with the royal court of the Joseon dynasty.

Its characters and stories draw from traditional Korean motifs, including the crane, rabbit, tiger and deer. Their roles reflect occupations and ways of life that Shin Haedal personally respects and admires.
