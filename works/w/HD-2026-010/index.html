POV : Paid HolidayPOV : 유급휴일

목심지태나전칠기(천연자개, 옻칠, 한지 등) · 27 × 27 cm · 2026Traditional Korean najeonchilgi on a hanji-covered wood core (natural mother-of-pearl, lacquer, hanji, etc.) · 27 × 27 cm · 2026

달력 속 파란 날, 토요일이다. 얼빵해달은 그럴싸한 계획이 있었지만 그냥 누워서 폰만 하며 보냈다. 근로기준법 제55조에서는 근로자에게 1주에 평균 1회 이상의 유급휴일을 보장한다.A blue day on the calendar — Saturday. Spaced-out Sea Otter had plans, sort of, but ended up just lying down, scrolling its phone. Under Article 55 of the Labor Standards Act, every worker is guaranteed at least one paid holiday per week, on average. This is what that guarantee looks like, exercised to the fullest.
