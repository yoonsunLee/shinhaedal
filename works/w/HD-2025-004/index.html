nn-th Degree Derivative Works ann차적 저작물a

목심저피나전칠기(천연자개, 옻칠, 삼베 등) · 15 × 15 cm · 2025Traditional Korean najeonchilgi on a hemp-reinforced wood core (natural mother-of-pearl, lacquer, hemp, etc.) · 15 × 15 cm · 2025

자개의 근원인 '조개'를 원형(Original)으로 삼아, 해체와 재구축을 통해 본질로 환원한 시리즈다. 2차적 저작물로서 끊임없이 확장되는 예술의 생명력과 창작의 순환을 담았다.Taking the shell — the origin of all mother-of-pearl — as the Original, this series reduces it to its essence through deconstruction and reconstruction, capturing the endlessly expanding vitality of art as a derivative work, and the ongoing cycle of creation.
